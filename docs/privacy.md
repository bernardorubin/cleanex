# Privacy Policy

**CleanEx** · Last updated 1 August 2026

## The short version

CleanEx does not collect anything about you. There is no account, no sign-up,
and no backend. Your photos and videos never leave your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing.

CleanEx has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party
data collection. We do not know who you are, we cannot see your photos, and we
have no server that stores anything about you.

## Your photos and videos

CleanEx asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates,
screenshots and large files you may want to delete. That access is used
entirely on your device, through Apple's own PhotoKit and Vision frameworks.

- Your photos and videos are **never uploaded**, copied off the device, or
  transmitted anywhere.
- Nothing is deleted unless you choose to delete it.
- Anything you delete goes to **Recently Deleted** in Apple's Photos app, where
  it stays for 30 days and can be recovered. CleanEx cannot remove it from
  there — that is under your control in the Photos app.

If you play a video that Apple's iCloud Photos has moved off your device to
save space, iOS fetches it from your own iCloud account so it can play. That is
between your phone and Apple. CleanEx is not involved and receives no copy.

## The only network connection

CleanEx checks whether a software update is available when it starts. That
check sends the app version and the device platform (iOS) and nothing else — no
personal information, no photo data, no identifying details about you.

That is the only network request the app makes. There is nothing else to send,
because nothing about you is collected in the first place.

## Storage permissions

CleanEx reads how much total and free space your device has, so it can show
you how full your phone is. This is a device-level figure. It is displayed on
screen and never transmitted.

## Children

CleanEx is a storage utility with no social features, no user content
sharing, and no data collection. It is suitable for all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page
with a new date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect meaningful
changes.
