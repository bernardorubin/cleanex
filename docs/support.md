# CleanEx — Support

## Getting help

The best way to report a problem or ask a question is to open an issue:

**[github.com/bernardorubin/cleanex/issues](https://github.com/bernardorubin/cleanex/issues)**

It helps to include which iPhone you have, which version of iOS it is running,
and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

### I deleted photos but my phone did not get any emptier

That is normal, and nothing has gone wrong. When you delete photos on an
iPhone, they move to **Recently Deleted** in the Photos app, where they wait 30
days in case you want them back. They still take up space until then.

To get the space back straight away: open **Photos**, tap **Albums**, scroll to
**Recently Deleted**, then **Select** and **Delete All**.

### Can CleanEx clear my WhatsApp photos?

Only the ones WhatsApp has saved into your Photos app — and by default it saves
every photo and video people send you, so that is usually a large pile.

The media kept inside WhatsApp itself is out of reach. iOS does not allow any
app to look inside another app's storage, so no app on the App Store can do
this, whatever it claims. CleanEx's **Guides** tab walks you through clearing
it yourself in WhatsApp, and measures how much space you got back.

### What is "System Data" and can CleanEx clear it?

System Data is caches, logs and temporary files that iOS keeps for itself. It
grows and shrinks on its own and often looks alarmingly large. No app can clear
it — iOS does that automatically when your phone runs low, and restarting your
phone often shrinks it. Any app claiming it can clean System Data for you is
not telling the truth.

### I deleted something by accident

Open the **Photos** app, tap **Albums**, and scroll down to **Recently
Deleted**. Anything deleted in the last 30 days is there and can be recovered.

### Does CleanEx cost anything, or collect my data?

No to both. It is free with no subscription, no adverts and no in-app
purchases, and it collects nothing about you. See the
[privacy policy](privacy).

### Why does it ask for access to all my photos?

To find duplicates and large files it has to be able to see the library. That
scanning happens entirely on your phone — nothing is uploaded, and nothing is
deleted unless you choose it. You can change or revoke access at any time in
**Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → CleanEx**.
